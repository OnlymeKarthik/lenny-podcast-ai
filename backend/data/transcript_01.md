# Lenny's Podcast: Episode 1 - Growth Tactics for B2B SaaS

Guest: Jane Doe, VP of Growth at Acme Corp

Lenny: Welcome to the podcast, Jane! Let's dive right in. What is the number one growth tactic you recommend for early-stage B2B SaaS companies?

Jane: Thanks for having me, Lenny. The biggest lever I've seen is focusing relentlessly on activation. Too many founders obsess over acquisition—getting people to sign up. But if your product is a leaky bucket, that acquisition spend is wasted. You need to identify the "Aha!" moment in your product and reduce the time to value. For us at Acme, we found that if a user invited a teammate within the first 3 days, they were 80% more likely to retain.

Lenny: That's fascinating. How did you operationalize that?

Jane: We completely restructured our onboarding flow. Instead of showing them a generic product tour, the very first screen asked them what their primary goal was. Based on their answer, we dropped them right into a personalized workflow that forced them to invite a teammate to complete it. We also used targeted email sequences. If day 2 passed without a teammate invite, they got a plain-text email from the CEO offering help.

Lenny: Plain text? Not beautifully designed HTML emails?

Jane: Exactly. Plain text emails feel personal. HTML emails feel like marketing. The open and reply rates on the plain text emails were 3x higher. 

Lenny: What about pricing? Does that impact activation?

Jane: Absolutely. We shifted from a freemium model to a 14-day reverse trial. In a reverse trial, you give them all the premium features for 14 days, and then downgrade them to the free tier if they don't buy. Loss aversion is a powerful psychological trigger. It drove our conversion rate from 2% to 6%.
